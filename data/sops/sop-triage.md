# SOP-TRIAGE — Incident intake

Every device alarm is an incident. Classify before acting.

P1 (15–30 minutes): fire, access control failure, chiller plant trip, any safety interlock.
P2 (60–120 minutes): single camera degraded, AHU comfort, robot docked unexpectedly.
P3 (4 hours): EV charger offline, cosmetic IR flicker with privacy mask intact.

The operator must retrieve the matching SOP, look up the device id, and only then open a work order. Do not reboot safety systems from chat.

Night IR flicker on lobby cameras is P2 unless the privacy mask is missing, in which case treat as P1 privacy incident.
