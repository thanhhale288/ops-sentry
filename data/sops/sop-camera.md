# SOP-CAMERA — Video, privacy, ANPR

Lobby cameras (CAM-014 class) must keep the lift-lobby privacy mask enabled. If the mask is off, treat as a P1 privacy incident, restrict live view to the DPO, and open a work order.

Garage ANPR (CAM-021) may store resident plates only. Do not export plate databases through this agent.

Night IR flicker with the mask intact is P2. Reboot is allowed only after confirming the mask in the last still frame.
