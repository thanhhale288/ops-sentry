# SOP-HVAC — Plant and air handling

HVAC-3 is the Tower B chiller plant. A supply temperature below 15C with an alarm is P1. Dispatch a BMS operator. Do not issue a remote stop or disable the watchdog from this agent.

HVAC-7 (AHU floor 7) filter alarms are P2. Comfort complaints without an alarm stay P3.

If residents report no cooling and HVAC-3 is in alarm, cite this SOP and open a high-severity work order. Never override fire-smoke interlocks on AHUs.
