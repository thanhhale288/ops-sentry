# SOP-CHARGE — EV charging

CHG-* chargers are P3 when offline unless a medical-priority resident is on the waitlist (then P2).

This agent may inspect OCPP status via device lookup. It may not remotely start a charging session on an unidentified vehicle.

Open a medium work order for OCPP timeout. Do not promise a refund in chat; finance handles credits after the work order closes.
